## 🐾 Mascotas (prompt único para Roblox Studio AI / Cube 3D)
```
Generate a collection of 15 cute low-poly cartoon pet characters for a Roblox
hide and seek game. All pets share the same art style: chubby round bodies,
big expressive eyes, smooth simple geometry, bright saturated colors,
Roblox-compatible scale.

Arrange them in 5 rows by rarity tier, 3 pets per row.

ROW 1 — COMÚN: Mini Ratón (tiny mouse, gray fur, pink nose), Pollito (yellow
chick, round fluffy body, orange beak), Tortuguita (small green turtle, star
shell pattern)

ROW 2 — LEGENDARIO: Búho Dorado (golden owl, amber eyes, feathered crown),
Lobo Plateado (silver wolf cub, icy blue eyes, white chest fur), Zorro Sombra
(dark purple fox, violet eyes, shadow trail)

ROW 3 — MÁXIMO: Dragón Bebé (chubby baby dragon, teal scales, tiny wings,
fire breath), Fénix Carmesí (small phoenix, red-orange fiery feathers, ember
particles), Tiburón Fantasma (ghost shark, translucent white body, glowing
blue fins)

ROW 4 — ULTRA: León Cósmico (lion cub, galaxy-pattern mane, stars floating),
Serpiente Rayo (electric snake, yellow body, lightning crackling), Oso Mecánico
(robotic bear cub, chrome plating, orange visor eyes, steam from joints)

ROW 5 — DIOS: Unicornio Divino (white unicorn foal, rainbow horn, aurora
wings, sparkle trail), Dragón Celestial (white and gold scales, multiple
glowing eyes, divine light aura), Gato del Vacío (pure black body, galaxy
swirl pattern inside, floating, reality-distortion effect)

Style: low poly cartoon, under 3000 triangles each, Roblox studio compatible,
bright base colors + one glow/particle effect per rarity tier, idle animation:
gentle floating bob
```
